RFC 2142, "Mailbox Names for Common Services, Roles and Functions" (D. Crocker, May 1997)
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2142.txt
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Only the 15 mailbox names the RFC specifies are used (abuse ftp hostmaster info marketing news
noc postmaster sales security support usenet uucp webmaster www). They are standards facts, not
copyrightable expression; no RFC text is reproduced. The RFC's own status notice reads:
"Distribution of this memo is unlimited." (RFC 2142 predates the IETF Trust Legal Provisions and
carries no other licence statement.)
